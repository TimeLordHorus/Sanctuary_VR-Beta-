# Sanctuary VR - Product Roadmap
## From Virtual Reality to Mixed Reality Gamified Life Experience

**Vision**: Transform Sanctuary VR from a peaceful VR environment into a comprehensive mixed reality platform that gamifies personal growth, wellness, and life experiences while maintaining the sanctuary's core peaceful philosophy.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Pillars

### 1. **Mixed Reality Integration**
Blend physical and digital worlds seamlessly

### 2. **Life Gamification**
Turn daily activities into engaging, rewarding experiences

### 3. **Wellness & Growth**
Support mental, physical, and spiritual development

### 4. **Social Connection**
Build meaningful communities and relationships

### 5. **Creative Expression**
Enable unprecedented creation and self-expression

## 🎮 Gamification Mechanics Deep Dive

### Core Loop
```
Real Life Activity → Track Progress → Earn Rewards →
Level Up → Unlock Features → Enhanced Experience →
Motivated to Continue → Real Life Activity
```

### Daily Engagement
**Morning Ritual** (15 minutes):
1. Wake up in virtual sanctuary
2. Guided meditation session
3. Review daily quests
4. Set intentions
5. Earn "Morning Glory" bonus

**Throughout Day**:
- AR reminders for breaks
- Quick meditation sessions
- Habit check-ins
- Social connections
- Learning moments

**Evening Ritual** (20 minutes):
1. Day review and reflection
2. Gratitude practice
3. Journal entry
4. Social time with community
5. Preparation for tomorrow
6. Sleep mode activation

### Progression Systems

**Character Growth**:
- **Level 1-10**: Novice (basics, tutorial)
- **Level 11-25**: Apprentice (building habits)
- **Level 26-50**: Adept (mastery begins)
- **Level 51-100**: Master (advanced features)
- **Level 100+**: Sage (mentor others)

**Skill Trees**:
```
Mindfulness Tree:
├── Meditation Mastery
│   ├── Breathing Techniques
│   ├── Body Awareness
│   ├── Thought Observation
│   └── Advanced States
├── Emotional Intelligence
│   ├── Self-Awareness
│   ├── Self-Regulation
│   ├── Empathy
│   └── Social Skills
└── Present Moment Living
    ├── Mindful Eating
    ├── Mindful Movement
    ├── Mindful Communication
    └── Flow States
```

### Quest Examples

**Daily Quest**: "Mindful Morning"
- Meditate for 10 minutes
- Complete gratitude journal
- Reward: 100 XP, 50 Crystals

**Weekly Challenge**: "Social Butterfly"
- Connect with 5 friends
- Attend 1 community event
- Help another user
- Reward: 500 XP, Rare cosmetic

**Life Quest**: "Fitness Journey"
- Exercise 3x/week for 3 months
- Milestones at 1 month, 2 months
- Final Reward: Epic achievement, special sanctuary area
